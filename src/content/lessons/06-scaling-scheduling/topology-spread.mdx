<Intro>
  <p>Topology spread ensures even Pod distribution across zones or nodes. More flexible than anti-affinity for complex spreading requirements. Controls the "skew" between domains.</p>
</Intro>

<table class="example-table">
  <tbody>
    <FileHeader filename="topology-spread.yaml" />
    <Row last>
      <p slot="docs">Topology spread is defined in <code>topologySpreadConstraints</code>. <code>maxSkew</code> is the allowed imbalance. With 6 Pods across 3 zones and maxSkew=1, each zone gets 2 Pods.</p>
      <Fragment slot="code">
```yaml
spec:
  topologySpreadConstraints:
    - maxSkew: 1
      topologyKey: topology.kubernetes.io/zone
      whenUnsatisfiable: DoNotSchedule
      labelSelector:
        matchLabels:
          app: my-app
```
      </Fragment>
    </Row>
  </tbody>
</table>

    <FileHeader filename="topology-spread-multi.yaml" />
    <Row last>
      <p slot="docs"><code>maxSkew: 1</code> means zones can differ by at most 1 Pod. <code>DoNotSchedule</code> enforces strictly; <code>ScheduleAnyway</code> is best-effort. Multiple constraints can have different strictness.</p>
      <Fragment slot="code">
```yaml
topologySpreadConstraints:
  - maxSkew: 1
    topologyKey: topology.kubernetes.io/zone
    whenUnsatisfiable: DoNotSchedule
    labelSelector:
      matchLabels:
        app: my-app
  - maxSkew: 2
    topologyKey: kubernetes.io/hostname
    whenUnsatisfiable: ScheduleAnyway
    labelSelector:
      matchLabels:
        app: my-app
```
      </Fragment>
    </Row>
  </tbody>
</table>

    <FileHeader filename="topology-spread-mindomains.yaml" />
    <Row last>
      <p slot="docs"><code>minDomains</code> ensures Pods spread across at least N domains. Prevents all Pods landing in one zone when cluster is small. Only applies with <code>DoNotSchedule</code>.</p>
      <Fragment slot="code">
```yaml
topologySpreadConstraints:
  - maxSkew: 1
    topologyKey: topology.kubernetes.io/zone
    whenUnsatisfiable: DoNotSchedule
    minDomains: 3
    labelSelector:
      matchLabels:
        app: my-app
```

    <FileHeader filename="topology-spread-taints.yaml" />
    <Row last>
      <p slot="docs"><code>nodeTaintsPolicy</code> controls whether tainted nodes are considered. <code>Honor</code> (default) excludes nodes with taints Pod doesn't tolerate. <code>Ignore</code> includes all nodes in calculation.</p>
      <Fragment slot="code">
```yaml
topologySpreadConstraints:
  - maxSkew: 1
    topologyKey: kubernetes.io/hostname
    whenUnsatisfiable: DoNotSchedule
    nodeTaintsPolicy: Honor
    labelSelector:
      matchLabels:
        app: my-app
```
      </Fragment>
    </Row>

    <FileHeader filename="topology-spread-affinity.yaml" />
    <Row last>
      <p slot="docs"><code>nodeAffinityPolicy</code> controls whether node affinity is considered in spread calculation. <code>Honor</code> only counts nodes matching affinity. <code>Ignore</code> counts all nodes.</p>
      <Fragment slot="code">
```yaml
spec:
  affinity:
    nodeAffinity:
      requiredDuringSchedulingIgnoredDuringExecution:
        nodeSelectorTerms:
          - matchExpressions:
              - key: workload-type
                operator: In
                values: [compute]
  topologySpreadConstraints:
    - maxSkew: 1
      topologyKey: kubernetes.io/hostname
      whenUnsatisfiable: DoNotSchedule
      nodeAffinityPolicy: Honor
      labelSelector:
        matchLabels:
          app: my-app
```
      </Fragment>
    </Row>

    <FileHeader filename="topology-spread-rollout.yaml" />
    <Row last>
      <p slot="docs"><code>matchLabelKeys</code> automatically selects Pods from the same controller (Deployment revision). Prevents spread calculation from including Pods from old ReplicaSets during rollout.</p>
      <Fragment slot="code">
```yaml
topologySpreadConstraints:
  - maxSkew: 1
    topologyKey: topology.kubernetes.io/zone
    whenUnsatisfiable: DoNotSchedule
    labelSelector:
      matchLabels:
        app: my-app
    matchLabelKeys:
      - pod-template-hash
```

Events:
